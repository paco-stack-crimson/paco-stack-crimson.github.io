Hi! I work with data about places: elections, neighborhoods, public spending and public security in Mexico. I build interactive maps and write analytical reports, mostly from official sources like INEGI, CONEVAL, SESNSP and the INE.

I run LAKEN, a public security and data consultancy. I studied Political Science and Political Economy at UC Berkeley and Geographies of Global Inequalities at Freie Universität Berlin.
