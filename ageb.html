La Laguna, block by block

TOOL

A geomarketing explorer for the Torreón–Gómez Palacio–Lerdo metro area, built on 332 urban census areas (AGEBs).

Each area carries its population and CONEVAL's social lag (rezago social) rating, and every registered business from INEGI's DENUE directory is plotted on top, about 127,000 establishments. Darker areas have higher social lag; dots are businesses.

About the data

Census figures from INEGI's 2020 census at AGEB level. Social lag from CONEVAL's 2020 urban AGEB index. Business points from DENUE, May 2026 release.

Sources: INEGI Censo 2020 · CONEVAL · DENUE
