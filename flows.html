MigData will be an open collection of migration statistics published by governments in the Global South, cleaned and made comparable across countries.

This preview maps flows into Mexico from nine countries in the region; line width is the size of the flow.

About the data

Planned sources include national migration institutes and statistical offices across Latin America, Africa and Asia. Launching 2027.
